HOW TO CHANGE PRODUCT IMAGES:
1. Open assets/products/
2. Replace product1.svg to product8.svg with your own product images.
3. If your image is JPG or PNG, update the image filename inside script.js.

HOW TO CHANGE POSTERS:
1. Open assets/posters/
2. Replace poster1.svg, poster2.svg, and poster3.svg with your own posters.
3. If you add more posters, copy another <img> line in index.html inside the poster-slider.
